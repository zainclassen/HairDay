Our Story & Culture

Meet Pria, Garry & The Hair Day Family

Pria earned her master certificate as a colorist and immediately set out to specialize in bold, custom color transformations. Driven by a desire to craft an intimate "treat yourself" sanctuary, she opened Hair Day alongside her good friend and veteran hairdresser, Garry.

Together, they built a cozy boutique environment where clients leave feeling refreshed, confident, and inspired.
